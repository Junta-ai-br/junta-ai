# Knowledge

This directory contains permanent project knowledge organized by domain.

Each subdirectory owns a specific type of canonical information.

---

## Domains

### Vision

Long-term project direction and purpose.

Answers: Why does this project exist? What problem does it solve? What is the desired future?

### Strategy

How the vision will be achieved.

Answers: What are our priorities? What should we build first? What should we avoid?

### Architecture

System organization, components, domains, boundaries and responsibilities.

### Decisions

Important project decisions and their rationale.

Answers: What was decided? Why was it decided? What alternatives were rejected?

### Features

Product capabilities and their specifications.

Answers: What should be built? Why does it exist? Who benefits?

### Research

Information collected before decisions are made.

Sources include articles, benchmarks, experiments, interviews and market analysis.
